# Sources

Every source used anywhere in `outputs/spotlight/`, what it's good for, how current it is, and
whether it's free or paid. Terry will add sources continuously — check this list before starting
new research so a source already known to be weak or stale isn't re-relied-on.

| Source | Good for | Currency | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Billboard official site (billboard.com) | Chart methodology, official chart data | Live/current | Free |
| Recording Academy (recordingacademy.com) | Grammy Week official programming, press releases | Live/current | Free |
| The Hollywood Reporter (hollywoodreporter.com) | Awards-season party guides, gifting-suite reporting | Current, dated articles | Free (some paywalled) |
| Variety (variety.com) | Awards-season party guides, trade news | Current, dated articles | Free (some paywalled) |
| Deadline (deadline.com) | Awards-season party lists | Current, dated articles | Free |
| PRNEWS (prnewsonline.com) | "Key Contacts" style booking-contact roundups | **UNRELIABLE FOR CURRENCY** — the "Key Contacts: Access Hollywood" piece used this pass had no visible publish date and reads as dated (fax-first booking preference). Treat as a lead requiring re-verification, not a confirmed contact. | Free |
| Event listing aggregators (Average Socialite, general event-calendar sites) | Confirming gifting-suite/satellite-event dates and locations | Moderate — good for dates, weak for access/submission routes | Free |
| Station social accounts (e.g. @KCBSKCALDesk) | Confirming a real, currently-monitored assignment-desk contact route | Good — an actively used account is a strong currency signal | Free |
