to catch: a shallower pass might have logged both as live targets and wasted a pitch on a show with

against the festival's own official site — about as solid as verification gets without a direct
phone call. Everything marked 🟡 or 🔴 above is a real, honest gap, not a placeholder — the next
pass should prioritize re-verifying the 🟡 entries (especially Kaci Sokoloff at CBS, a genuinely
high-value contact) before they're used in any real outreach.
