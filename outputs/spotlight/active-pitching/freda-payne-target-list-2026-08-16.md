## Pitch craft applied — what "lead with the buyer's need" means for each lane, concretely
- **Festivals:** lead with what fills the room and fits the lineup — her live draw and genre fit —
  not her personal career narrative.
- **AARP/editorial:** lead with the story their readers want — a "still creating, still evolving"
  arc — not a press-release-style credentials list.
- **Tamron Hall:** lead with the human story angle the show is built around, not a booking request.
- **Every lane:** the ask should be a conversation or an application, never presented as a
  confirmed booking or a foregone conclusion — consistent with `clients/freda-payne.md`'s own hard
  rule never to imply anything as confirmed before it is.
