## What it is, in plain English
Taste of Soul is the largest one-day free outdoor festival on the West Coast — produced by the
Los Angeles Sentinel (Bakewell Media) on Crenshaw Boulevard, reportedly drawing 300,000+
attendees. It has multiple stages covering R&B, soul, gospel, and hip-hop alongside food, wellness,
and community programming. This is not a niche booking — it's a major, high-visibility community
event with real press attention, which is exactly why it fits Freda's profile (legacy artist,
community-rooted appeal, gospel material in progress).

## Who programs talent, and the submission route — this is the real gap
There is a clear, public **vendor** application process (food, retail, nonprofit, service
providers) at `tasteofsoulla.net`, opening early in the year, with a dedicated "Become a Vendor"
page on the Sentinel site. **There is no equivalent public "performer application" process found
in this pass.** Search results describe performer/artist lineups as "announced closer to festival
day," which strongly suggests talent booking for the stages is curated directly by the event's
producers (LA Sentinel / Bakewell Media) rather than run through an open submission portal.
*(Source: [Taste of Soul — Become a Vendor — LA
Sentinel](https://lasentinel.net/taste-of-soul-2026/taste-of-soul-become-a-vendor))*
**Verified:** 2026-08-16.
