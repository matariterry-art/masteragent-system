The Week Hollywood and the UN Overlap

By Terry Bryant · PR STARPOWER Press Room · Beat 1: The Pulse · August 16, 2026

HOLLYWOOD, Calif. — Everyone tracking the fall access calendar is watching the same six-week stretch: Venice, Telluride, the Emmys, and Toronto, all overlapping September 2–20. Fewer people are watching what happens the week immediately after — and it may end up mattering more.

Three confirmed, dated events land in the same seven days, September 22–28, and none of them are on the standard Hollywood awards calendar:

  • The United Nations General Assembly's 81st session opens its High-Level Week on September 22, running through September 28.
  • The Global Citizen Festival returns to Central Park on September 26, with a lineup including Hugh Jackman, Ms. Lauryn Hill and Wyclef Jean, Lenny Kravitz, and John Legend.
  • The MTV VMAs return to Los Angeles on September 27 — the first time the show has been held there since 2017.

The connection worth drawing, and the point of this piece: this is not a second wave of the same festival-circuit access already crowding early September. It's a structurally different lane — diplomatic and humanitarian, not red-carpet — opening in the exact week the film-festival circuit is winding down. Firms and talent positioned only for the red-carpet lane will be competing hard for a shrinking pool of access during the first three weeks of September. Firms with a real diplomatic or humanitarian door — not a rented one — have a comparatively open field the week after.

That's not a hypothetical distinction. Access to UN-adjacent rooms runs through credentialed press and institutional affiliation, not through a publicist's usual channels — which is precisely why this window gets overlooked by firms built around the standard awards-season playbook.

Unverified/rumor: none in this piece — all three dates are confirmed from official or primary event sources.

Sourcing: UN High-Level Week dates via the United Nations' own General Assembly page; Global Citizen Festival lineup and date via Global Citizen's official festival page and CBS News New York; MTV VMAs date and venue history via Billboard and Entertainment Now.

What this means for PR STARPOWER: the founder's own journalism credentials and international press affiliations are a genuine structural advantage in exactly this window — this is the access lane most competing boutique firms cannot open at all, regardless of budget.
