## From 2026-08-16 (focused EPK brief — see that dated file for full detail and sources)
- EPK format shift: web-based living page as primary, condensed PDF as secondary/cold-email-only.
- Standard EPK section order: hook → bio → music/video → photos → press → dates → contact.
- Empty/thin sections should be cut, not left half-filled.
- Booker attention span (~30 seconds to decide, 30-60 seconds per track sampled).
- Common ignore-triggers: low-res/unclear photos, multi-attachment kits, generic mass pitches,
  outdated stats.
- Emerging (UNVERIFIED as established) trend: press kits built for AI-discoverability/AEO, not
  just human journalists.
- Legacy-artist EPK additions vs. new-artist EPK: real tour history with venue/capacity detail,
  numeric placements, label/publishing affiliations, a compiled career highlight reel, and a
  curated (not exhaustive) press section.
