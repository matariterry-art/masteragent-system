# PR STARPOWER — Media Contact & Outlet Architecture

**Run date:** August 16, 2026
**Built by:** media-contact-builder agent

## Privacy rule (absolute — applies to every line in this file)
This file stores **structure and categories only**. It never stores a named individual (no
producer names, no bookers, no journalist bylines as contact targets) and never records the
specifics of what was traded for a placement. Every entry follows the format:

> [outlet category] — [booking desk / role type], standard lead time [X], hook required: [angle type]

If a future update to this file is tempted to add a name or a trade detail, that update is out
of scope for this agent and should be refused.

## How this file is used
- `social-content-engine` reads this file plus `outputs/hollywood-calendar.md` to build its
  posting calendar and to know which outlet categories are realistic booking targets around
  which calendar dates.
- `market-signal-researcher` may also reference this for outlet-category context.

## 1. National daytime shows

| Category | Booking structure | Standard lead time | Hook required |
|---|---|---|---|
| National network daytime talk (syndicated & network-owned) | Central booking desk per show; segment producers assign by topic lane (lifestyle, interview, panel) | 2–4 weeks for a standard segment; 24–72 hours for reactive/news-hook bookings | Project premiere, milestone, exclusive story, or timely news angle tied to the day's hook |
| Syndicated daytime strip shows | Booking desk, often centralized across a station group | 3 weeks standard | Exclusive or milestone — syndicated desks are conservative on "no news" bookings |

## 2. Late night

| Category | Booking structure | Standard lead time | Hook required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Network late night (broadcast) | Talent booking department, segment producers by beat | 4–6 weeks for a panel/interview slot; project-driven (film/show release window) | Project in current release window; late night desks rarely book pure lifestyle/appearance slots without a project |
| Streaming/cable late night | Smaller booking team, often faster-moving | 2–4 weeks | Same as above, with more openness to cultural-moment or viral-hook bookings |

## 3. National morning shows

| Category | Booking structure | Standard lead time | Hook required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Network morning shows (national) | Segment-specific booking (entertainment desk vs. lifestyle desk vs. news desk) | 2–3 weeks for planned segments; same-day to 48 hours for breaking/reactive bookings | Premiere, tour/launch, exclusive interview, or a live news angle the desk is already covering |

## 4. LA local news

| Category | Booking structure | Standard lead time | Hook required |
|---|---|---|---|
| LA-market local news (affiliate entertainment desks) | Local assignment/entertainment desk, distinct from national bureau even at co-owned stations | 1–2 weeks; same-week possible for local-angle stories | Local tie-in (LA-based project, LA charity event, red-carpet actuality) — local desks want local relevance, not just national fame |

## 5. Podcasts

| Category | Booking structure | Standard lead time | Hook required |
|---|---|---|---| 
| A-list-tier interview podcasts | Direct booking relationship or agency-to-show booking contact; no centralized "desk" — varies by show | 3–6 weeks (many book several months out for top-tier shows) | Long-form story to tell — podcasts want narrative depth, not a 3-minute press-tour stop |
| Industry/trade-adjacent podcasts (trade press-affiliated shows) | Booking often runs through the trade outlet's own editorial team | 2–3 weeks | Industry-relevant news angle (deal, project, award-season context) |

## 6. Entertainment trade press (journalists and editors)

| Category | Coverage lane | Standard lead time for pitches | Hook required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trade dailies (film/TV industry news desks) | Deal news, casting, festival/awards coverage | Days, not weeks — trades run on news cycles | Genuine news: deal, casting, festival selection, award nomination |
| Trade awards-season columns/editors | Dedicated awards-circuit coverage during Sep–Mar | 1–2 weeks ahead of the specific calendar event (see `hollywood-calendar.md`) | Tie to a specific dated event on the calendar — trades cover the circuit on a fixed rhythm |
| Fashion/style editors covering red carpets | Red-carpet look coverage, gifting-suite roundups | 1–2 weeks pre-event for stylist/look coordination | A booked red-carpet appearance or gifting-suite presence (see calendar) |
| Business-of-entertainment / trade features desks | Longer-lead profile and trend pieces | 4–8 weeks | Distinct trend or business angle, not a single placement |

## 7. Gifting suites, brand activations, and ambassador/affiliate structures

Per CLAUDE.md standing rule 6, this business monetizes through access, barter, gifting, and
ambassador/affiliate deals as well as retainers — this section tracks the *structure* of that
access, not specific brand names or deal terms.

| Category | Access route | Standard lead time | Hook required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awards-week gifting suites/lounges | Suite operator application process, typically opens weeks before the tied award show (see calendar for tied dates) | 3–6 weeks before the suite date | No news hook needed for suite entry itself; suite presence becomes the hook for subsequent trade/style coverage |
| Luxury/fragrance brand partnerships | Direct brand relationship or agency-brokered ambassador deal | Varies widely — luxury cycles run seasonally (spring/fall collection tie-ins) | Brand-fit narrative (image alignment), not a single news event |
| Ambassador and affiliate programs | Structured multi-touch relationship (appearances, content, campaign cycles) tied to brand calendar, not the Hollywood calendar | Onboarding lead time typically 6–8 weeks before first activation | Long-term image fit; renewed periodically against campaign calendars |
| Brand activations at festivals (Venice, Telluride, TIFF, Sundance, PSIFF) | Brand-hosted lounges/events run parallel to official festival programming; access via brand relationship, not festival credential | 4–6 weeks pre-festival for placement coordination | Festival presence (official selection, jury, or red-carpet slot) strengthens the ask but is not always required for brand-hosted event access |

## 8. International / diplomatic and humanitarian lane (structural note, not a contact list)

Per CLAUDE.md standing rule 11, the founder's journalism credentials and international/UN-connected
affiliations are a structural access advantage into diplomatic, humanitarian, and global cultural
circles that standard Hollywood publicists cannot reach. This is noted here as a **category of
access**, not a contact list: UN-connected and humanitarian-gala circuits should be tracked as
their own outlet/venue category in future calendar updates, run on a different rhythm than the
Hollywood awards calendar (see `outputs/hollywood-calendar.md`, December holiday-gala section for
the one instance flagged this cycle). No individual names or affiliations are recorded here, per
the privacy rule.
