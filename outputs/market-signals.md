## 2. Competing boutique Los Angeles publicist rates

- **Signal:** Industry pricing guides (not primary trade press, but consistent across multiple
  independent sources) put boutique LA/entertainment PR retainers at roughly $2,500–$15,000/month,
  with emerging-talent-focused boutiques starting around $3,000–$5,000/month and elite/established
  representation running $10,000+/month. Broader boutique-agency benchmarking (not LA-specific) puts
  the boutique band at $3,500–$15,000/month generally, moving toward $50,000+/month only at top-tier
  global-firm scale.
  **Source:** AMW pricing guide (amworldgroup.com/pricing/publicist-cost), Everything-PR 2026 pricing
  guide, ShapiroPR Los Angeles cost breakdown, Mission Driven Brand 2026 pricing breakdown.
  **Date observed:** Aug 16, 2026.
  **Why it matters:** PR STARPOWER's visibility retainer ($3,500/month) and active retainer
  ($6,500/month) sit squarely inside the boutique band and are priced *below* the ceiling most
  boutiques charge for comparable scope — room exists to hold or increase active-retainer pricing
  without moving outside competitive range, especially once the signed client's A-list access
  (red carpet, festival, UN-adjacent) is positioned as differentiated rather than generic press
  relations. **Caveat:** these are aggregate pricing-guide figures, not confirmed rate cards from
  named competing firms (no boutique publicist publishes a public rate card) — treat as directional,
  not exact.

## 4. Platform and algorithm shifts affecting creator visibility

- **Signal — Instagram:** Instagram rolled out its most significant ranking overhaul since Reels
  launched, beginning Q1 2026. Adam Mosseri confirmed watch time, profile clicks, and saves are now
  primary distribution signals, with follower count "significantly reduced" as a quality proxy.
  Reported organic reach for accounts not producing those signals fell from a 2025 average of
  28–42% of followers to 10–20% in 2026. Instagram also launched user-facing "Your Algorithm" controls
  (rolled to English-speaking users Jan 2026, after Reels-only testing from Oct 2025) and is actively
  suppressing repost/unoriginal-content accounts in favor of original creation.
  **Source:** Reporting citing Mosseri's public statements, aggregated across MeetEdgar, Hootsuite,
  and multiple 2026 algorithm-tracking blogs (secondary sources summarizing Meta statements — Meta's
  own newsroom post was not independently located in this search, so treat the *magnitude* figures
  as **UNCONFIRMED** even though the directional shift toward watch-time/saves is corroborated across
  independent outlets).
  **Date observed:** Aug 16, 2026 (rollout dated to Q1 2026 in sourcing).
  **Why it matters:** This directly affects the "digital branding and social media strategy" service
  line — follower count is no longer a credible pitch metric to brands or media; content quality/watch
  time is. Relevant both for the signed client's owned Instagram (per CLAUDE.md standing rule 7,
  Instagram takes priority over the website) and for evaluating prospective influencer-crossover
  clients, where engagement-per-post now matters more than raw following.

- **Signal — TikTok:** TikTok's 2026 algorithm is reported to weight completion rate and rewatches
  over follower count (favoring nano/micro creators on reach-per-post), and to weight commerce signals
  (product saves, TikTok Shop taps, purchase completions) more heavily in For You Page distribution
  than in 2024. A "Don't Show on TikTok Profile" test feature allows posting content that reaches
  non-followers without appearing on the public grid.
  **Source:** Aggregated secondary reporting (Stackinfluence, OpusClip, mickyweis.com) citing TikTok's
  own "algorithm outlook" content; TikTok's own discover-page content was located but functions more
  as platform-authored marketing copy than a formal newsroom release — **treat specifics as
  UNCONFIRMED**, directional shift toward completion/commerce signals is consistent across sources.
  **Date observed:** Aug 16, 2026.
  **Why it matters:** Reinforces that a smaller, highly engaged following is now a legitimate pitch
  asset for influencer-crossover prospects — relevant to vetting inbound interest from creators who
  may have large but passive followings versus smaller, high-completion accounts.

  **Date observed:** Aug 16, 2026.

  unrepresented by traditional entertainment boutiques, which skew film/TV/music. No confirmed direct

- **AI/virtual influencers — flagged as a competitive and vetting signal, not a service line:**
  Reported brand adoption of virtual personas is climbing (one source cites 60% to 73% year-over-year,
  with reported engagement 3x human creators). **Source:** SQ Magazine, Metricool, croudx.com —
  marketing-content aggregation; **figures UNCONFIRMED**, no primary source (e.g., an ad-industry
  trade outlet) independently verified in this pass.
  **Why it matters:** Relevant less as an opportunity and more as context for brand budget
  allocation — if a meaningful share of ambassador/affiliate budget is shifting toward synthetic

## 11. The creator economy — how influencers earn, what they pay for, rising categories

- **Signal:** The Influencer Marketing Factory's 2026 Creator Economy Report (based on 5M creator

  brand alignment over one-off campaigns. 56.1% of US creators believe AI will significantly change how
  they work in the next few years.
  **Source:** theinfluencermarketingfactory.com 2026 Creator Economy Report (PDF), cross-reported by
  Tubefilter and Digital Information World.
  **Date observed:** Aug 16, 2026 (report published earlier in 2026, per Tubefilter's March 2026
  coverage).
  **Why it matters:** This is the most directly load-bearing finding for the influencer-crossover
  division. It confirms (a) most working creators are still under-earning relative to their reach and
  actively seeking stability/brand-alignment over quick deals — a clean match for a retainer or active-
  retainer pitch rather than a one-off booking — and (b) creators overwhelmingly expect AI to reshape
  the field, which argues for positioning "Hollywood access and polish" as the differentiator a creator
  cannot get from an AI tool or a self-service platform deal.

  between traditional PR response and viral spread) is consistent with widely reported industry
  dynamics but not independently quantified here.**
  **Date observed:** Aug 16, 2026.
  **Why it matters:** This is structural support for PR STARPOWER's flagship crisis positioning
