## Ranked angles

### 1. The "new chapter" pairing — LEAD WITH THIS ONE

**The angle:** a legacy soul and jazz artist, six decades in, already the subject of one
well-received memoir, now moving toward gospel music and a second book at the same time. Presented
as an exclusive first-look conversation, not a release announcement — "here's what's next," not
"here's what's out."

**Why this is the strongest option, ranked first:** it's the one angle with zero existing press
coverage anywhere (confirmed absent in the press-history research in the campaign plan), it
requires nothing external to be confirmed first — no booking, no date, no third party — and it's
actionable today. Every other angle either depends on an unconfirmed event or competes with an
already-saturated press pattern.

**Outlet built for:** EURweb's Gospel desk (`eurweb.com/category/entertainment/music/gospel/`) —
already identified and pitched once as a real test run (see
`outputs/media-desk/2026-08-16/bespoke-pitches/eurweb-gospel-desk-pitch.docx`). Real, dedicated,
recurring editorial coverage of exactly this kind of legacy-artist gospel story — confirmed by
`global-spotlight`'s placement-pattern research as a stronger, more findable pattern than trying
to catch a trade-press beat reporter.

**What would make it stronger:** either property getting a real date. A dated hook turns "here's
what's coming" into genuine news, which opens broader outlets beyond a dedicated genre desk.

---

### 2. Taste of Soul, Los Angeles — the most concrete peg, but currently blocked

**The angle:** a legacy soul artist, deeply rooted in Black music history, at the largest
one-day free festival on the West Coast — with her gospel direction as a natural extension of the
festival's own programming mix (R&B, soul, gospel, hip-hop, per `booking-desk`'s research).

**Why it's ranked second, not first, and stated honestly:** this is genuinely the most concrete,
dated peg available — October 17, 2026, confirmed — but her appearance is still **pending
outreach**, not confirmed, per `clients/freda-payne.md` and Wave 1 item 1.10 of the campaign plan.
Pitching press coverage of an appearance that hasn't been secured yet would be premature and could
create a public expectation the founder hasn't cleared. This angle activates the moment that
booking is confirmed — not before.

**Outlet built for:** LA local press and Black press specifically — this is a community-rooted,
LA-specific story, not a national trade pitch.

**What would make it reachable:** securing the Taste of Soul booking itself. This is a sequencing
dependency, not a research gap — the story can't be told before the event is real.

---

### 3. "Still evolving" — the reinvention angle, real but harder to land

**The angle:** positioning her not as a nostalgia act but as an artist whose career has moved
through genres for decades — soul, jazz standards, and now gospel — a "still growing" story rather
than a legacy-hit retrospective.

**Why it's ranked third:** it's a real, legitimate angle, but it's the least differentiated of the
three. Per the press-history research in the campaign plan, her jazz pivot (the Ella Fitzgerald
tribute shows) is already actively covered — this angle risks reading as more of the same rather
than something new, unless it's framed specifically around the gospel move as the newest data
point in that pattern.

**Outlet built for:** broader entertainment-news formats rather than a genre desk — e.g., an
R&B/legacy-artist-friendly interview podcast like R&B Money (per `booking-desk`'s earlier
research). **Not** Access Hollywood, despite it fitting the format — `global-spotlight` flagged
its only researched contact route as currency-unreliable (an undated secondary source with a
dated-sounding fax-first booking preference); using it now would mean pitching a contact that
likely no longer works.

**What would make it stronger:** framing it explicitly around the gospel move specifically, not
the jazz pivot that's already been told — otherwise this risks being the angle that gets a
polite pass for sounding familiar.

---

## Direct recommendation

**Lead with Angle 1.** It's the only one of the three that's both genuinely fresh and immediately
actionable — no external confirmation required, no risk of getting ahead of an unconfirmed
booking, and no competition with an already-told story. Angle 2 becomes the second move the moment
Taste of Soul is actually secured. Angle 3 is worth holding in reserve, sharpened around the gospel
framing specifically, rather than leading with it as currently scoped.
