## August 16, 2026

- **PRLog** — free press release hosting/distribution, with optional paid upgrades for extra
  visibility/SEO. Supports images, video, and embedded links in a free listing.
  *(Source: [Top Free Press Release Distribution Services — eReleases](https://www.ereleases.com/pr-fuel/free-press-release-distribution/))*
- **Reality check, not just a tool listing:** general reporting on free distribution sites is
  consistent that they rarely produce real media pickup, traffic, or measurable reach on their
  own — useful as an SEO/backlink layer or an official public record of a release, not as a
  substitute for the direct, personalized outreach this agent's bespoke lane is built around.
  **What this means for PR STARPOWER:** a free distribution service is worth using as a
  supplementary step after real outreach, not instead of it — it shouldn't be mistaken for a
  pitching strategy.
