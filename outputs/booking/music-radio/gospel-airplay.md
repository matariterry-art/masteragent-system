## How this chart actually works — the mechanism, in plain English
Billboard's Gospel Airplay chart is not a popularity contest based on streams or press. It is
built from radio spins **electronically detected** at a defined panel of gospel-format stations,
monitored around the clock by a system called Mediabase, with the resulting data supplied to
Billboard by Luminate (the same company that supplies Billboard's sales/streaming data). A station
playing the song does nothing for the chart unless that station is *on the monitored panel*.

**Panel size:** one source (Devine Jamz Gospel Network's own FAQ page, via search summary — not
independently verified) cites **656 Gospel-format station contacts** in the Mediabase/Luminate
reporting network. Treat this as a directional figure, not a confirmed current count — panel
composition changes, and this needs verification directly with Luminate/Mediabase or a promoter
before being used to set expectations with Freda.
*(Sources: [Gospel Airplay chart](https://www.billboard.com/charts/gospel-airplay/), [National
Radio Airplay FAQ — Devine Jamz](https://www.devinejamz.com/top-christian-radio-airplay-faq/))*

## Lead time — sources disagree, confirm directly
- General industry guidance (2026 independent-artist radio promotion overview): dedicated radio
  promo campaigns typically run **$1,800–$2,400 for six to eight weeks**, with pitching to radio
  recommended **3–4 weeks before release** so adds land in the first week.
- A separate, more general source on independent radio campaigns (not gospel-specific) suggested
  campaigns starting **6–8 weeks pre-release**, with **60–90 days** needed to build sustained,
  chart-relevant airplay.
  **Why flag the discrepancy instead of picking one number:** these came from different sources
  covering different genres and campaign types. The real lead time depends on which promoter is
  hired and what package is bought — this is a question to put directly to whichever promoter
  Freda's team selects, not something to lock in from secondary sources.
*(Sources: [Radio Promotion for Independent Artists: 2026 Guide —
Chartlex](https://www.chartlex.com/blog/marketing/radio-promotion-independent-artists-2026),
[How to Get Radio Airplay Independently](https://orionpromotion.com/radio-airplay/))*
