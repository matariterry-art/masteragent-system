## What this document is, and isn't

This is the single, sequenced version of everything researched across Parts 1 through 6 of the
companion plan — the single's radio/chart timeline, the book's long-lead press timeline, the
combined-campaign logic, brand and fashion mechanics, television and casting mechanics, and warm
access. Those parts explain *why* the mechanics work the way they do; this document exists to show
*in what order* to actually move, and specifically **which door opens which other door** — because
that causal chain, not any single tactic, is the actual strategy here.

**Two hard constraints carried forward from every prior section, restated because this document
sequences real-looking dates:** the "Wave" windows below are internal planning horizons for PR
STARPOWER's own work — they are not, and must never be read or repeated as, a public release date
for the single or a publication date for the book. Neither property has a confirmed date as of this
writing. Nothing in this document should be shown to press, brands, or casting contacts as a
confirmed date or a booking. Anything marked "founder decision" is a genuine open item — this
document does not guess at it.

**How to read the waves:** Wave 1 is foundation only — it does not land a brand deal, a fashion
week seat, or a TV booking. It makes those things *possible* later. Wave 2 only exists because of
what Wave 1 produces — every item in Wave 2 states which Wave 1 output it depends on. Wave 3 is
where the actual outside targets (brand deals, fashion week, national talk, DWTS) become live
conversations — and only because Waves 1 and 2 did the work of making PR STARPOWER's pitch, and
Freda's platform, real enough to survive contact with a decision-maker.

## Wave 1 — Next 90 Days (roughly August 16 – mid-November 2026): Foundation, Not Landing

**Purpose of this wave, stated plainly:** nothing in Wave 1 is a brand deal, a fashion week seat, or
a TV booking — this wave exists to remove the structural gaps that Parts 1–7 identified as the
reason none of those are reachable yet. Every one of those parts traced its "not yet" back to the
same handful of missing things: a dated release, a documented platform, current materials, and a
complete team. Wave 1 is where those get built or decided.

### 1.1 Lock single production decisions
- **What has to be true first:** nothing — this is the first domino. Recording, mixing, mastering,
  and distribution are all currently blank per the client file.
- **Who's approached:** a producer/studio (if not already engaged) and a distributor (the service
  that delivers the finished track to streaming platforms and into Luminate's tracking system, per
  Part 1).
- **What the founder supplies:** a decision — who the producer/studio/distributor are, and
  confirmation of current mixing/mastering status. This cannot be researched or assumed; it's the
  single most load-bearing open item in the whole plan, because nearly everything in Waves 2 and 3
  depends on a real single release existing.

### 1.2 Set an actual target release date for the single, once production is locked
- **What has to be true first:** 1.1 complete (a finished or near-finished master).
- **Who's approached:** no one yet — this is an internal decision.
- **What the founder supplies:** a confirmed target date (or a firm window). Per Part 1, this date
  then determines when radio servicing (1.3) needs to start.

### 1.3 Engage an independent gospel radio promoter
- **What has to be true first:** a real target release date (1.2), since promoter engagement needs
  to happen roughly 6–8 weeks before that date per Part 1's researched radio-lead-time findings.
- **Who's approached:** an independent gospel radio promotion firm (a category of vendor identified
  in Part 1's research, not vetted or recommended by name here).
- **What the founder supplies:** a decision on budget and which promoter to hire — vetting and
  hiring is explicitly flagged in Part 1 as `booking-desk`'s intended territory once that agent

### 1.4 Set a target publication window for the book (a season is enough)

### 1.5 Fill the Team section of the client file

## Wave 2 — Months 4 through 9 (roughly mid-November 2026 – May 2027): What Wave 1 Makes Possible

This wave does not run on its own timeline — every item below exists because a specific Wave 1

## The door-opens-door chain, stated plainly

This is the single causal thread running through all three waves, worth stating once, directly:

**A confirmed single release date and a confirmed book publication window (Wave 1, founder
decisions) → unlock a real radio campaign and a real long-lead press calendar (Wave 1/2) → which
produce actual dated coverage and a chart run (Wave 2) → which, combined with a documented social
platform and refreshed materials (also built in Wave 1), give brand marketing teams and TV casting
scouts the "why her, why now" they structurally require before they'll engage (Part 4, Part 5, Part
6) → which is what makes a real brand conversation, a fashion week presence, and a DWTS-adjacent
opportunity reachable in Wave 3 — and, separately, is also the exact material a warm relationship
(Part 6) needs in hand before it's worth spending.**

Every "not yet reachable" finding across Parts 4, 5, and 6 traced back to the same root cause: no
dated news hook and no documented platform existed yet. That is precisely what Wave 1 is designed to
fix, which is why this plan treats the single's release date and the book's publication window —
still both unconfirmed as of this writing — as the two decisions the entire year's sequencing
actually pivots on.
